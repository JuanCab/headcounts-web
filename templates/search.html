Advanced URL Navigation Guide

All navigation can also be done by editing your browser's URL directly.

Key Rules:

  • The first part of the URL after the site name is one of:
    • a subject (e.g. phys or econ) — shows courses in that subject (any matches a course number across all subjects)
    • a college code: cbac, coah, cshe, cehs, or none
    • a course type: all, lasc, wi, or 18online
  • Narrow results by adding (up to two) of:
    • a course number (append _ for wildcard, e.g., 241_)
    • an LASC area number (if you selected lasc)
    • a year-term code (use all to return all terms)

Examples

  • Current Math courses: {{ url_for('index', _external=True) }}math
  • College of Business courses: {{ url_for('index', _external=True) }}cbac
  • Writing-intensive courses: {{ url_for('index', _external=True) }}wi
  • Math 261: {{ url_for('index', _external=True) }}math/261
  • Math 261, Fall 2014: {{ url_for('index', _external=True) }}math/261/20153
  • CSIS 349L, Fall 2024: {{ url_for('index', _external=True) }}csis/349l/20253
  • MUS 156 (any variant), Fall 2024: {{ url_for('index', _external=True) }}mus/156_/20253
  • Art courses, Fall 2014: {{ url_for('index', _external=True) }}art/20153
  • LASC area 2, Spring 2016: {{ url_for('index', _external=True) }}lasc/2/20165
  • All courses, all years: {{ url_for('index', _external=True) }}all
